Tired of unwelcome guests in your Ferndale home? Sasquatch Pest Control works
across Ferndale and the rest of Whatcom County, and the plan we put together
depends on the property rather than on a package number.

## Treatment built for the property

Our pest control work is set up to deal with the problems Ferndale homes
actually get, on a schedule that reflects the pest and the season rather than a
fixed calendar.

## Family-run, working locally

We are a family-run business working out of Bellingham, a few minutes down the
Guide from Ferndale, and that is where every truck starts its day. It means the
person who assesses the house is the person who comes back to it.

## No inspection or cancellation fees

We do not charge for the assessment visit or for canceling. The one exception
is bed bugs, where confirming an infestation takes a verification visit that
carries a fee — and that fee is credited toward the treatment if you go ahead.

## Ready to evict some pests?

Get in touch and we will come out, take a look at the property and tell you what
the work involves and what it will cost before anything is scheduled.

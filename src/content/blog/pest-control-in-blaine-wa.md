## Extermination work

When pests get inside, the work has to be done properly rather than quickly. We
handle a range of household pests and address infestations on a schedule that

## Rodent control

Our rodent work covers homes and businesses, dealing with the animals present
and then with the gaps that let them in. Rodents cause damage and carry health
risks, and exclusion — closing the entry points — is the part that keeps the
problem from returning.

## Termites and wood-destroying pests

Termites and the other wood-destroying organisms in this region work through
structural timber slowly and out of sight. We treat for dampwood and
subterranean termites, carpenter ants, moisture ants and wood-boring beetles.
Treatment protects the structure, and the sooner it happens the less there is to
repair.

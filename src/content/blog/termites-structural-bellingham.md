## Signs of wood damage from pests

Caught early, wood-destroying pests are very manageable. Watch for:

- Hollow or crumbling wood
- Sagging or springy floors
- Frass or sawdust piles
- Discarded insect wings
- Damp, musty crawlspace
- Carpenter ants indoors

## Common questions

### Do we really get termites in Washington?

Yes, but they're typically dampwood termites, and activity here is closely tied to moisture. Subterranean termites — the species most national articles are written about — are uncommon in Whatcom County. Control the moisture and seal the structure, and you address the problem at its root.

### What's the difference between termites and carpenter ants?

Both damage wood, but they do it differently and they're treated differently. Termites eat wood; carpenter ants excavate galleries to nest in it, strongly preferring wood that moisture has already softened. Carpenter ants are considerably more common in this county than termites are, and they fall under household bug and insect control services.

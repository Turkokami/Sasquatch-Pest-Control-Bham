## What is Integrated Pest Management (IPM)?

IPM is a science-backed pest control strategy that focuses on prevention first, not just spraying chemicals.

Instead of using pesticides as the first solution, we:

- Take a careful look around your home
- Identify pest activity and entry points
- Target the root cause of infestations
- Use non-chemical control methods whenever possible

Chemicals are used only when necessary — and when they are, we choose products that are targeted, low-toxicity, and applied with precision.

## Why eco-friendly pest control matters for families

Traditional pest control can rely heavily on broad-spectrum chemicals. While effective, overuse of these products can create unnecessary exposure risks for:

- Children who crawl and play on floors
- Pets who groom themselves and explore areas close to the ground
- Beneficial insects and local pollinators

Our eco-friendly pest control services are designed to protect what matters most while still delivering real results.

## Common questions

### Why do I have so many ants and spiders in the Northwest?

Our wet, mild climate is ideal for moisture ants, odorous house ants, and a wide range of spiders. Targeting entry points and moisture-prone areas, rather than just spraying, is what keeps the numbers down over the long run.

### Are your treatments safe for kids and pets?

Yes. Our technicians use targeted, professional products applied precisely where pests travel and harbor, following all label and safety guidance.

### Do I need recurring service or a one-time treatment?

Both options are available. One-time visits address an immediate problem, while recurring service is the more effective approach for managing the year-round moisture pests that come with this climate.

The Peace Arch stands where Blaine meets the border, a monument to the unity
between two countries that share this stretch of coast. It is the landmark
Blaine is known for, and the reason a lot of people who do not live here have
still stood in the park beneath it.

## A monument that links two sides

What the arch represents is straightforward: two nations, one crossing, and a
long stretch of shared history along the shoreline. It links the two sides
rather than marking a division between them, which is unusual for a structure
built on an international border and is most of why it endures as a symbol.
